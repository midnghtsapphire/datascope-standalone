# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Position
DataScope is an intelligence-first security platform focused on clarity, speed, and operational trust.

## Voice and Tone
- Clear, direct, evidence-based
- Professional and operationally focused
- Avoid hype; emphasize measurable outcomes

## Messaging Pillars
1. Faster threat visibility
2. Actionable intelligence (not raw noise)
3. Production-ready workflows for teams

## Visual Direction
- Primary palette: deep slate/blue with high-contrast accent for alerts
- Typography: modern sans-serif for readability in dashboards
- UI principle: dense but readable cards, tables, and severity highlighting
