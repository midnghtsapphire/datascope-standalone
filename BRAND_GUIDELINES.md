# Brand Guidelines

## Brand position

DataScope Standalone is positioned as a practical, AI-enabled intelligence platform for teams that need actionable data outcomes quickly.

## Voice and tone

- Clear
- Credible
- Action-oriented
- Technical without unnecessary jargon

## Messaging pillars

1. **Collect broadly** — gather data even when APIs are unavailable.
2. **Analyze intelligently** — derive insights with AI-assisted engines.
3. **Report decisively** — generate outputs suitable for operators and executives.

## Core value statement

“From raw multi-source data to decisions in one workflow.”

## Visual direction

- Primary emphasis on clarity and trust
- Dashboard-first presentation style
- Consistent typography and high-contrast UI for operational readability
